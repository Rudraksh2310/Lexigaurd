WEBSITE DESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 2 November 2026 (the "Effective Date") at Pune, Maharashtra.

BETWEEN

Kavya Iyer, an independent web designer, residing at 21 Lakeview Apartments, Aundh, Pune 411007 (the "Designer");

AND

Chai Point Cafe LLP, a limited liability partnership, having its office at 8 MG Road, Camp, Pune 411001, represented by its partner Mr. Arjun Mehta (the "Client").

The Designer and the Client are together called "the Parties".

1. Purpose
The Client wants a new website for its cafe, and the Designer agrees to design and build it on the terms set out in this Agreement.

2. The Work
The Designer will design and build a five-page website (Home, Menu, About, Gallery and Contact) that works on mobile phones and computers. The Designer will deliver the finished website files and set the website up on the Client's hosting account. The Work includes two rounds of changes after the first design is shared. Any further changes will be agreed in writing, with the price for them, before the Designer starts on them.

3. Timeline
The Designer will share the first design by 20 November 2026 and deliver the finished website by 15 December 2026. If the Client takes longer than five working days to give feedback or provide content, the delivery date moves by the same number of days. Either Party will inform the other promptly if it expects any delay.

4. Fees and Payment
The total fee for the Work is Rs. 60,000 (Rupees Sixty Thousand only). The Client will pay 50% when signing this Agreement and the remaining 50% within 30 days of delivery of the finished website. The Designer will send a written invoice for each payment. The fee stays fixed for the Work described in clause 2 and can be changed only by a written agreement signed by both Parties. If the Client disagrees with any invoice, it will tell the Designer in writing within 7 days and the Parties will resolve it in good faith.

5. Client's Responsibilities
The Client will provide the text, menu details, photographs and logo for the website by 10 November 2026, and confirms that it has the right to use them. The Client will give feedback on each design within five working days.

6. Ownership of the Work
Once the Client has paid the full fee, the Client will own the final website design, text and graphics created specifically for it under this Agreement. The Designer keeps ownership of her general design tools, templates and know-how that existed before this Agreement or that are not specific to the Client, and grants the Client a permanent, free licence to use any of them that form part of the website. The Designer may show the finished website in her portfolio after it goes live, unless the Client asks her in writing not to.

7. Private Information
Each Party will keep the other's non-public business details, such as recipes, supplier prices and passwords, private, and will use them only for the Work. This does not apply to information that is already public or that a Party must disclose by law. This duty lasts for two years after this Agreement ends. When the Work is complete, the Designer will delete the Client's passwords and return or delete any files the Client provided.

8. Quality and Support
The Designer will do the Work with reasonable skill and care. For 30 days after delivery, the Designer will fix, free of charge, any errors in the website that she caused.

9. Ending the Agreement
Either Party may end this Agreement by giving the other 15 days' written notice. Either Party may also end it with 7 days' written notice if the other Party seriously breaches this Agreement and does not put it right within those 7 days. If the Agreement ends early, the Client will pay for the Work done up to the end date, in proportion to the total fee, and the Designer will refund any amount paid beyond that and hand over the Work completed so far.

10. Limits on Responsibility
Each Party's total responsibility to the other under this Agreement is limited to the total fee payable under clause 4, except for fraud or deliberate wrongdoing. Neither Party is responsible for losses that the other Party could not reasonably have expected when signing this Agreement.

11. Events Outside a Party's Control
Neither Party is responsible for a delay caused by events outside its reasonable control, such as natural disasters, epidemics or a long internet outage, as long as it informs the other Party promptly and resumes the Work as soon as it can.

12. Relationship of the Parties
The Designer works as an independent professional and not as an employee of the Client. The Designer is free to work for other clients.

13. Changes to this Agreement
This Agreement can be changed only in writing, signed by both Parties.

14. Resolving Disagreements
If a disagreement arises, the Parties will first try to settle it by talking to each other in good faith. If it is not settled within 30 days, either Party may refer it to mediation in Pune, with the cost shared equally. If mediation does not settle it, either Party may approach the courts at Pune.

15. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.

16. Entire Agreement
This Agreement is the complete agreement between the Parties about the Work.
