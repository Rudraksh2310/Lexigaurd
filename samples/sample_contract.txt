MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Section 1. Term and Renewal.
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of twelve (12) months. Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive terms of one (1) year each, unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days prior to the expiration of the current term.

Section 2. Fees and Invoicing.
Customer agrees to pay all fees within net 15 days of invoice date. Vendor reserves the right to impose a late payment penalty of 1.5% per month on overdue balances. Additionally, all fees are subject to change without notice and additional fees may apply for standard maintenance.

Section 3. Termination.
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience without cause upon ten (10) days notice. Vendor may terminate immediately in its sole discretion without refund of prepaid fees.

Section 4. Intellectual Property.
Customer agrees that all work product, source code, designs, and deliverables created under this Agreement shall be work made for hire, and Customer hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in such intellectual property to Vendor.

Section 5. Indemnification and Defense.
Customer shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor and its officers, directors, and employees from and against any and all claims, liabilities, losses, damages, and reasonable attorney fees arising out of the performance of this Agreement.

Section 6. Limitation of Liability.
Except for gross negligence, in no event shall Vendor be liable for any indirect or consequential damages. Notwithstanding any other provision herein, Customer shall have unlimited liability for any and all claims arising under this Agreement.

Section 7. Restrictive Covenants.
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twenty-four (24) months following termination, Customer shall not solicit, recruit, or hire any employee or contractor of Vendor, and shall not compete directly or indirectly with Vendor's core business offerings.

Section 8. Dispute Resolution.
Any controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this contract shall be settled by binding arbitration in accordance with commercial arbitration rules. The parties waive the right to a jury trial.
