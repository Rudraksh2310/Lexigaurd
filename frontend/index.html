From upload to a clear next step

Each document goes through the same five steps, in about the time it takes to make tea.

  1. It checks the file is a legal document

    A resume or an invoice is turned away with the reason, so you never get made-up red flags.

  2. It marks the risky clauses

    Red for serious, orange for worth negotiating, yellow for things to know. Judged from your side: employee, tenant, customer, freelancer.

  3. It explains each one

    Hover a highlight to see what the clause means, why it matters, and what to ask for. You can also have it read aloud in English or Hindi.

  4. It answers your questions

    Ask about any part of the document. Answers point to the exact clause and suggest fairer wording you can propose.

  5. You keep a report

    Download a PDF or Word copy with the highlights and every explanation, to share or take to a lawyer.

Ask it like you'd ask a friend who reads contracts

"Can they keep my original degree?" "What happens if I leave in month eight?" "Rewrite the bond clause so it's fair." Every answer is based on your document and points to the clause it comes from.

Can they really keep my original degree certificate?

Clause 5 #4 says the Company keeps your original certificates until the two-year bond ends. That makes it hard to leave or apply elsewhere, and it's a common point to push back on.

Ask to show the originals for verification and hand over self-attested copies instead.
